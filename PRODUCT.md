# PRODUCT.md — Dr. Tanja Pia Metelko

## register
brand

## Product Purpose
Personal brand marketing website for Dr. Tanja Pia Metelko — Slovenia's leading transformational coach. Replaces existing Wix site at spremenitesvojum.si. Primary goal: position Tanja as the lead character (not Joe Dispenza or HeartMath). Dual audience: B2C individuals seeking personal transformation, and B2B executives/HR commissioning corporate programs.

## Users
- B2C: Slovenian-speaking adults 30-55 seeking personal growth, coherence, meaningful change
- B2B: Executives, HR directors, COOs at Slovenian companies commissioning transformation programs

## Brand Voice
Calm precision. Editorial gravitas. Confident, declarative sentences. No exclamation marks. No "vibracije/energija" new-age clichés. Mix Slovenian elegance with executive briefing tone. Avoid em-dashes.

## Strategic Principles
- Tanja is the brand. Joe Dispenza and HeartMath are credentials.
- Lead with Zavestni Kreator (her flagship authored synthesis), then HeartMath, then NCS.
- "Mir kot strateška prednost. Koherenca kot kompetenca prihodnosti." is the anchor tagline.
- Credibility metrics: 20+ let prakse, 25.000+ posameznikov, 300+ podjetij, 21 retreatov.

## Anti-references
- No new-age softness, crystal energy, abundance-magnet language
- No Dispenza-dominated visual hierarchy
- No auto-rotating testimonials, popups, exit modals, video backgrounds
- No stock-photo carousels, fake trust logos, particle effects
- No Inter, Roboto, Arial or system fonts
- No pure white (#fff) or pure black (#000)
